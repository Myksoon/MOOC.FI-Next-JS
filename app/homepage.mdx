# Welcome to Blog App

Blog App is a place to collect and share interesting blog posts. Add the posts
you find worth reading, like the ones others have shared, and keep a reading
list of what you still want to read.

## Features

- **Create and Manage Blogs:** add blogs with a title, author and URL, like them, and search them by title
- **User Profiles:** see every user and the blogs they have added, and keep your own reading list
- **Authentication:** register and log in with a username and password

## Getting Started

1. [Register](/register) a new account
2. [Log in](/login) with your username and password
3. [Create a new blog](/blogs/new)
4. [Browse all blogs](/blogs) and like your favourites
5. [See the users](/users) and what they have added
